# Reference video breakdown: *Left Left Left!*

Source: **4 TikTok clips from [@leftleftleftgame](https://www.tiktok.com/@leftleftleftgame)**, sent on
2026-10-04. Each is 13–21 s, vertical (576×792), 30 fps. Every frame was pulled at 2 fps and the key
ones are in [`reference/`](reference/). (A fifth clip, a Minecraft mod video, was sent by mistake and
is ignored.)

## What the game is

- ***Left Left Left!*** is a cartoony **2-player co-op rally game**. Both players sit **in the same
  car, both in first person**: the driver on the left, the navigator on the right.
- The navigator holds a **rugged orange tablet** that shows the road ahead as a line. They yell the
  turns and the driver has to trust them.
- **Status, checked 2026-10-04:** I found **no Steam page, store page or press coverage** under that
  name. Most likely it isn't out yet and the developer is building an audience on TikTok first.
  Not verified: who makes it, the price, the release date, whether it has an AI navigator.

## Element by element

**Cameras and UI**
- Both players are first person. You can turn your head (free look).
- **No HUD at all.** The only number on screen is the stage timer, and it's *on the tablet*.
  Everything is in the world (a "diegetic" UI).

**The navigator's tablet** (`03-tablet-closeup-and-driver-helmet.jpg`)
- Orange rugged case with chunky corner handles, held with both hands.
- Dark screen. A top bar shows the timer as `m:ss,t` (`0:00,0`).
- The road is a **pale yellow line**, the car an **orange triangle** near the bottom. It's
  **heading-up**: it turns with the car and scrolls forward.
- It shows maybe ~150–300 m ahead (my estimate from the curves). There's no terrain, trees or
  hazards on it, just the line.
- **The real trick:** looking down at the tablet means you aren't looking at the road. The
  information split comes from **where you look**, not from fog.

**The driver**
- **Red full-face helmet with a visor**, red race suit, chunky low-poly arms. The hands follow the
  steering wheel.

**The car** (`01-driver-view.jpg`, `10-free-look-side-window.jpg`)
- A boxy **red '80s hatchback / sedan**, left-hand drive.
- Grey dash with vents and a radio slot, black steering wheel, gear lever with a red knob, door
  mirrors, red bars inside (probably a roll cage), tan/orange seats.

**The world**
- A narrow **gravel / dirt road** with grass edges.
- Dense **low-poly forest**: pines, round leafy trees, white-trunk birches, bushes. Open meadows.
- Bright blue sky, soft light, light haze in the distance.
- No spectators and no other cars seen.

**The calls**
- Casual, **not** numbered pacenotes: "LEFT", "RIGHT!", repeated for urgency ("LEFT! LEFT!
  LEFT!"), "WIPER!".

**The art style**
- Flat-shaded low poly, saturated greens, a red hero car, chunky arms. No outlines.

**Not seen in the clips (unknown)**
- Menus, car choice, damage, scores, penalties, more than one stage, an AI navigator, how mud gets
  on the glass.

## What we take vs. what we make ours

Copying a game's *idea and rules* is legal: the US Copyright Office says copyright doesn't protect
"the idea for a game… or the method or methods for playing it". Copying their **art, name, logo or
exact UI** is not okay, so we don't.

| We take (the idea) | We make ours |
|---|---|
| Both players in one car, first person | **Our own name:** SHOTGUN RALLY |
| The split comes from where you look (map vs. road), and the map shows only the road and you | **Our own map device:** a wooden map board, not an orange tablet |
| Casual yelling works ("LEFT! LEFT!") | **Real co-driver jobs** on top of calling: see [`CO_DRIVER_JOBS.md`](CO_DRIVER_JOBS.md) |
| Mud on the windshield + wipers | **AI co-driver** for solo, plus a quick-call wheel |
| Forest gravel stages, low-poly look | **Recce mode** (write your own notes), an optional pro pacenote mode, crews racing each other later |
