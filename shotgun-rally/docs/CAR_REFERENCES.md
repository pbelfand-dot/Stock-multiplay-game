# Car references

Photos sent as examples for the cars, their bodies, liveries, wheels and interiors. The game
makes **look-alikes** (its own names, no real sponsors), and the paint came first (the garage's
liveries); new bodies come later. This is what each reference is for.

## Bodies and liveries

| Car | What to take from it |
|---|---|
| **Ford Puma Rally1** (Red Bull colours) | a modern Rally1 shape: wide arches, big rear wing, dive planes; dark blue / red / yellow livery |
| **BMW E30 M3** (rally, red-and-white sponsor scheme) | boxy 3-box coupé, flared arches, a tall rear wing, red mud flaps, gold multi-spoke wheels; a white roof, red lower body, gold pinstripe bands down the sides and across the boot (the GOLDLINE livery's look) |
| BMW E30 M3 (touring car, white with the M stripes) | the blue / violet / red stripes down the bonnet and sides, white wheels |
| BMW E30 M3 (all red with big yellow lettering) | a single-colour car with huge sponsor type; gold BBS mesh wheels |
| **Audi Quattro A1 / A2** | long-bonnet coupé, box arches, a banked row of four to six big round driving lamps on the front; white with brown and red panels (the QUATTRO livery), or white and yellow with red pinstripes; plain white or silver five-spoke wheels |
| Audi Sport Quattro S1 | the short-wheelbase evolution: white / yellow / red |
| **Mercedes 190E** (black, gold and red pinstripes, #41) | a saloon in black with fine gold / red lines |
| **Mercedes 450 SLC** rally coupé | long, low coupé: silver with a black bonnet, four white lamp pods on the bumper, black arch extensions, mesh guards over the headlights, wire-mesh (BBS) wheels; or blue-and-white with fine pinstripes (a raid car) |
| **Lancia Stratos HF** | the wedge: tiny wheelbase, wraparound screen, a roof spoiler, louvres over the rear deck, a bar of four lamp pods on the nose; white with green and red chevrons (the TRICOLORE livery's look), yellow wheels, grey mud flaps |
| **Subaru Impreza WRC** (1997-99) | the 4-door shape with the big rear wing, a roof scoop, bonnet vents, a gravel snorkel; blue with the yellow swoosh and stars (the COMET livery's look), gold wheels, blue mud flaps |

## Tyres, wheels, underbody

- **Gravel tyres:** Hoosier and Pirelli Scorpion gravel rally tyres (tall sidewalls, deep blocks)
  on black and on white wheels.
- **Underbody:** an upside-down Subaru, the arrow on the sump guard / bash plate: what you see
  when a car rolls (a flat aluminium skid plate, the exhaust, the diffs and the arms).

## Interiors (already used)

Lancer Evo, a Mk2 Escort, Subaru STI and Impreza WRC, Lancia 037: stripped cabins, the cage,
bucket seats, harnesses, the sequential lever and hydraulic handbrake, the switch panel, a
deep-dish suede wheel (cockpit.gd, driver_hands.gd, harness.gd).
