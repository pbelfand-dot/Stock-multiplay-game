# Credits: the cars

Generated from the vehicle registry by `tools/vehicle_credits.py` (don't edit by hand).
SHOTGUN RALLY is a game for friends: several models below are game rips or carry
non-commercial licences. **Before any public or paid release**, every row with a
provenance note or a NonCommercial / unconfirmed licence must be replaced or cleared, and
the real makes' names and badges (in the registry's `real` and in some textures) settled.
Licence texts and attribution files from the packages are kept with the restored packages
(`assets/cars_restored/`, outside the build); the inventory, with each candidate's evidence,
is `docs/VEHICLE_PROGRESS.md`.

## Everything else

- Engine sounds: markeasting/engine-audio (MIT code; its recordings: see `tools/engine_sound/README.md`);
  the electric car's motor, turbo whistles, blow-off and supercharger whine are made in code.
- The game's own cars (KESTREL, COMET, VORTEX, MARTELLO), the crew's seats and the loading art: Higgsfield,
  from the owner's account. The crew: the owner's Claude Racer character (Mixamo-rigged).
- Co-driver voice: Higgsfield Seed Audio 1.0, preset voice "Fraser". Font: Oswald (SIL OFL).
- The driver's gloves (`assets/models/gloves/gloves.glb`): Higgsfield, from the owner's account: a photo
  made with GPT Image 2.5 (a red and white racing glove gripping a bar), made 3D with Tripo H3.1, fitted
  to the steering wheel rim by `tools/fit_glove.py` (Blender).
- Wheels (`assets/wheels/`, `tools/import_wheels.py`; all CC0): the 17 in five-spoke and 225/45 R17 tyre
  from the [Innerscene Parts Library](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/17-inch-five-spoke-alloy-wheel-and-tyre-x3d-50c20c60);
  the CC96 rims by [IceMaan](https://icemaan.itch.io/free-low-poly-car3-3-body-kits-4-rims); the wheels of the
  [USD Working Group's USD_Mini_Car_Kit](https://github.com/usd-wg/assets/tree/3b75c2dad6a494897557dcca0098257bcf42a8c6/full_assets/Vehicles/USD_Mini_Car_Kit).
  The garage's other wheel types are built in code (`scripts/vehicle/wheel_gen.gd`), their names made up.
