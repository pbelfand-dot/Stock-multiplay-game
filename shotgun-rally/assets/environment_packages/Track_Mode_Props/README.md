# Track-mode pit and marshal props

Fourteen CC0 GLBs selected from the 3DAssets.dev Formula and endurance packs:

- tyre stack in warming blankets and six-tyre warmer rack;
- front and rear quick-lift jacks plus an air-jack rig;
- pneumatic wheel gun with hose and gravity fuel rig;
- pit gantry lights, pit-wall monitor stand, and double-bay pit garage;
- helmet equipment rack;
- straight tyre-stack safety wall;
- marshal observation hut and blank flag on a pole.

All models are original, generic, metre-scale GLB 2.0 assets under CC0 1.0 Universal. They
were generated with AI tools by their upstream contributor (Claude Fable 5.1); none carries
a `No AI` restriction. No external textures are required because the GLBs are self-contained.

Sources and exact integrity data are in `../../TRACK_MODE_ASSET_MANIFEST.tsv`. The complete
upstream JSON records are preserved in `../../car_packages/Track_Mode_Cars/Formula/` and
`../../car_packages/Track_Mode_Cars/GT3/`.

These are source models. Their presence here does not imply that collisions, interactions,
animation, or track placement have been implemented.
