## Kenney

- City Kit (Roads): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads
- License: https://kenney.nl/support

Kenney publishes the City Kit (Roads) under CC0. It is best used for modular road layout, intersections, signs, traffic lights, collision foundations, and distant geometry; higher-detail materials can be layered over it for the final realistic look.
